# NO[CTRL]

In a time when a radio tower took over humanity's minds, you embody a deteriorated robot whose purpose is to shut down the tower and restore consciousness to organic minds. Make your way through a journey filled with obstacles and challenges.

Keep in mind: many years spent alone while humanity was unconscious has left you in a rough shape. Be aware of your failing locomotion system.

This is a game being developed by students from <a href="https://www.imagecampus.edu.ar/">Image Campus</a>
